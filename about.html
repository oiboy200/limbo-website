About
Limbo.

A frame is a boundary, but also an invitation to look closer.

Limbo Cornici is an independent framing workshop based in Vicenza, Italy. We bring together a considered eye for images, a love for materials and the precision of hand-finished work.

From a single artwork to a complete space, every project begins with listening. We study proportions, colours, finishes and details to create a frame that belongs to what it holds.

Small details make the difference. We believe in taking the time to get them right.
